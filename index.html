About Us

Safeguard Environments is an investment and operating firm focused on acquiring and improving small businesses and real estate assets. We take a hands-on approach to ownership by prioritizing operational discipline, financial transparency, and long-term sustainability across every platform we oversee.

Meet the Team

Founder & Principal

Isaac Weber

Isaac Weber is the Founder and Principal of Safeguard Environments. He oversees asset management, operations, and the strategic direction of the firm, ensuring each platform is built with discipline and durability in mind.

Under Isaac's leadership, Safeguard has grown to more than $3 million in assets under management. Prior to founding the firm, Isaac gained direct development experience on projects exceeding $20 million in aggregate value across the Midwest.

Isaac holds dual Bachelor of Science degrees in Construction Management and Construction Safety Management from UW-Platteville and served as a volunteer firefighter during his college years.
